# SalarySeed privacy policy

*Last updated: 14 August 2026. Applies to SalarySeed v1.0.0 for iOS.*

## The whole of it

**SalarySeed does not collect anything.** Your salary, your job, your município,
your household situation and every other answer stay on your iPhone. They are
never uploaded, never shared, and never seen by anyone but you.

This is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. There is no data
to handle: the app has no server, no account, no login, and no code in it that
could send your answers anywhere.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter lives in the app's own storage on your device: your salary
and how you are paid, your allowances, your município, your sector, job, time at
your employer, age band, education and gender if you chose to give them, your
household situation for the tax calculation, your name if you entered one, and
your language and colour preferences.

All of it is deleted when you delete the app.

## What leaves your phone

One thing, and it is not about you.

When you open the support screen, the app asks Apple for the current price of the
€4.99 supporter unlock, and if you buy it, Apple handles the purchase and tells
the app whether you own it. That traffic is between your device and Apple. It
carries nothing from the app: no salary, no profile, no identifier of ours.

**Apple is the seller.** We never see your Apple ID, your name, your email or any
payment detail. What we receive from Apple is an anonymous monthly sales total.

Apple's own handling of that purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not
this one.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDK or framework. The app is built entirely on Apple's own
  frameworks and has no dependencies.
- No location access. The app asks which município you live in and never asks
  your device where it is.
- No access to contacts, photos, calendar, health data or any other permission.
  The app requests no permissions at all.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and asks about employment and pay. It has
nothing to offer anyone below working age.

## Your rights

Since nothing about you ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to show you,
correct or delete. You already hold all of it, and deleting the app deletes all
of it.

If you want to be certain of that, the app's App Store listing shows "Data Not
Collected" under App Privacy, which Apple requires to be accurate.

## Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, it will say so on screen, ask before
doing it, and this document will change first. The App Store privacy label would
change at the same time.
